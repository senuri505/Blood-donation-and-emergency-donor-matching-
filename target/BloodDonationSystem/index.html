About HemoLink

Reinventing Emergency Blood Donation Supply Chains

Traditional blood banks often face critical shortages during emergencies. HemoLink bridges the gap by directly connecting nearby eligible donors with hospitals requesting urgent transfusions.

Our Mission

Zero delay in emergency blood procurement

We empower healthcare professionals with precise location and blood-compatibility matching while offering voluntary donors a simple, dignified way to save lives safely.

  • Real-time donor availability status tracking
  • Automated 56-day donation cooldown timer enforcement
  • 100% free and open platform for hospitals & donors

Safety & Privacy First

Verified medical compliance

Donor data confidentiality is protected. Contact details are only shared between verified hospital personnel and participating donors for active blood donation requests.

  • Strict hospital license verification by system administrators
  • Donor health eligibility screening (Age min 18, Weight min 50kg)
  • Direct notifications without public contact exposure
Simple 4-Step Process

How HemoLink Works

A seamless digital workflow designed for emergency efficiency from registration to donation completion.

01

1. Register Account

Donors register with blood group, age, weight, and location. Hospitals register with facility license credentials.

02

2. Get Approved

Admin reviews and approves new registrations to guarantee authentic donors and accredited medical facilities.

03

3. Search & Request

Hospitals search compatible donors by blood type and city, sending targeted donation requests with patient details.

04

4. Donate & Respond

Donor receives alert, views hospital details, accepts request, and completes the blood donation process.

Platform Features

Built for Donors & Medical Facilities

Tailored interfaces for voluntary blood donors and emergency hospital administrators.

For Donors

Save lives on your terms

  • Availability Toggle: Turn availability on/off anytime with one click.
  • Matched Alerts: Receive notifications when hospitals need your blood type.
  • Accept/Decline Requests: View patient urgency and hospital details before confirming.
  • Donation History & Cooldown: Automatic tracking of total donations and 56-day cooldown countdown.

For Hospitals

  • Direct Request Dispatch: Send urgent requests directly to targeted compatible donors.
  • Donor Response Tracking: Monitor live donor acceptance/decline responses.
